# StreetCraft: Local Growth Engine for Physical Businesses

**StreetCraft is a local growth engine for physical businesses.**

It helps cafes, restaurants, and neighborhood businesses turn a **real business opportunity** — such as a quiet afternoon, new dish, weekend offer, festival, or customer moment — into a coordinated campaign that customers can actually see and act on.

---

### In One Sentence

> **StreetCraft turns one local business opportunity into everything customers need to see.**

---

### What It Does

A business gives StreetCraft its permanent context:

- What the business sells
- Where it is located
- Who it serves
- Its local area & neighborhood
- Its brand voice & tone
- Signature offers and hero products
- Busy and quiet time windows

Then, when an opportunity arises:

```text
Business opportunity
        ↓
   StreetCraft
        ↓
   One campaign
        ↓
Google Search & Maps
Instagram
WhatsApp
In-Store Print
```

The outputs are coordinated around the **same offer, timing, audience, and business context**.

---

### What StreetCraft Is Not

StreetCraft is **not**:

- A generic copywriter
- A prompt generator
- A social media scheduler
- A marketing agency
- A generic CRM
- Another analytics dashboard
- A collection of generic templates

AI is part of the engine, but **AI is not the product being sold**.

---

### What the Customer Is Actually Buying

Store owners are buying a direct answer to:

> **"I have an opportunity at my business today. What should I put in front of my customers?"**

StreetCraft turns that question into an immediate, executable campaign.

---

### The Fundamental Product Idea

> **One opportunity &rarr; one campaign &rarr; multiple customer touchpoints.**

This core definition serves as the immutable foundation for the entire StreetCraft product, web platform, pricing, and user experience.

---

## Architectural Mapping

### 1. Permanent Store Preferences (`/app/business`)
- Stores immutable business context: neighborhood, signature items, target customer persona, brand vibe, and quiet slump hours.

### 2. Proactive Opportunity Intelligence (`/app/dashboard`)
- Daily morning briefing highlighting relevant immediate moments (slump windows, upcoming weekend crowds, local festival dates).

### 3. Campaign Composer (`/app/create` & `/free-tool`)
- Outcome-driven generator compiling one opportunity into coordinated formats:
  - Google Business profile update
  - Instagram Post, Story sequence & Reel hook
  - WhatsApp broadcast customer copy
  - In-store printable counter card / table tent proof

### 4. Campaign Vault (`/app/campaigns`)
- Persistent archive of generated proofs, active schedules, and walk-in results.
